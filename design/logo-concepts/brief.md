# SpotiStats — concept brief

SpotiStats helps a listener recognize their own musical taste through top tracks, artists, recent plays, and listening patterns. The identity should feel personal, curious, precise, and playful, alongside the existing dark, Spotify-green Kokonut interface.

Assumptions: Angel is the decision-maker; the name remains **SpotiStats**; this is an independent listening dashboard; first uses are its header, favicon, and app avatar. Keep the interface's green available, but judge form in black and white first. No change to the website logo until a direction is chosen.

Success: a readable name, a compact symbol with a clear silhouette at 16px, one-color and reversed compatibility, and enough character to sit beside the animated UI. Avoid headphones, equalizer bars, generic charts, and Spotify's three curved lines.

## Word map and concept longlist

Listen → repeat, rhythm, groove, return. Personal → signature, fingerprint, preference, collection. Stats → reveal, selection, pattern, focus. Spot → pinpoint, notice, keep.

1. **Replay S — letterform:** a continuous S ribbon with matching 45-degree cutback terminals. Selected: clear initial, simple outline, directional energy.
2. **Tasteprint — abstract:** nested tracks interrupted by a single detached spot. Selected: a personal listening signature, beyond a literal music icon.
3. **Saved Spot — pictorial:** a bookmark whose side opens around one selected dot. Selected: the listening moments worth keeping.
4. Sleeve seam: two album sleeves reveal an S between them. Rejected: library-stack convention, weak at 16px.
5. Needlepoint: a stylus ends in a selected dot. Rejected: too close to record-player pictograms.
6. Repeat arrow: an S doubles as a circular arrow. Rejected: common playback-control language.
7. Rank fold: one folded chart column hides an S. Rejected: too much analytics shorthand.
8. Orbit spot: a dot travels around a listening orbit. Rejected: generic technology icon.
9. Double-S links: interlocking initials encode repeated listening. Rejected: crowded visual territory; reference library includes Squarespace.
10. Pulse wordmark: rhythmic spacing modifies the lettering. Rejected: weaker as a compact app symbol.

## Construction and references

All symbols use a 256-unit canvas, solid black fills, and real negative space. Replay uses 32-unit bands with circular returns and diagonal terminals. Tasteprint uses 32- and 24-unit tracks, with deliberate clear gaps. Saved Spot uses a solid tab and a 64-unit circular notch with a detached 24-unit dot.

The library was searched for music, loops, fingerprints, bookmarks, and negative space. Framer, Patreon, Kotlin, Pagekit, and TikTok were examined for geometric simplicity, figure/ground, silhouette, and category conventions, not copied. This is a preliminary visual comparison, not a trademark clearance.

The concept lockups use outlined **Manrope SemiBold (650)**, sourced from Google Fonts, licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. No installed font is needed to display the SVGs. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

Deliverables in this round: three symbols, three concept lockups, a grayscale overview, and visual/audit checks. Color systems, final variants, mockup boards, and icon kits follow selection.
