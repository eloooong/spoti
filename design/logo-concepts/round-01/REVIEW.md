# Concept review

The selected review artwork is `concepts.png` / `concepts.svg`. Editable symbol and outlined lockup files are the `*-v2-*.svg` files. The `v1` files retain the first iteration.

## Checks performed

- Generated and visually inspected two rounds of the three symbols and lockups.
- Ran the logo-design skill's SVG audit in both rounds. Refined symbol scores: Replay S 100, Tasteprint 99, Saved Spot 100. These are geometric heuristics, not a measure of originality or legal clearance.
- Viewed the generated HTML test sheets in the browser, including one-color/reversed versions, 16/32px rendering, the size ladder, application contexts, and a reference shelf.
- Inspected the grayscale overview after offline SVG-to-PNG rendering with resvg.
- Symbols contain filled vector paths, no strokes, no raster images, no filters, and no live text. Concept lockup lettering is outlined Manrope 650.

## Refinements

- A: retained the original geometry after small-size inspection; 32-unit bands and matching 45-degree terminals keep the S clear.
- B: aligned the detached dot to the track baseline. Its symbol sits 4 units above the canvas center optically; the audit's off-center note is intentional.
- C: opened the cutout from radius 32 to 36 and increased the dot from radius 12 to 16, preserving 16px recognition.

## Recommendation and tradeoffs

**A / Replay S** is the recommendation: immediate initial recognition, economical silhouette, and energetic terminals that fit the existing interactive UI. Its risk is the crowded category of geometric S marks; it needs a wider similarity review before a public launch.

**B / Tasteprint** feels more personal and exploratory. Its risk is that some people may read an arch or headphone before a fingerprint; the name lockup supplies the brand connection.

**C / Saved Spot** expresses selection and collecting. Its risk is that it communicates bookmarks or an editorial product more strongly than music.

These are concepts for selection, not a final identity kit. The website's existing identity remains in place. After a direction is selected, refine the final silhouette, prepare color/reversed variants and favicon artwork, and check similarity before public use.
